Field Manual

INFO

Survive the wave: every hostile downed is +10, every kill +50. Enemies chase and shoot on sight — keep a wall between you and them while you line up shots. Grab yellow ammo pickups (walk over them, or press E nearby), and reload with R before you're empty. Clear the wave to advance; each wave spawns more enemies, with more health, moving faster. Sound effects are generated live in your browser; the arena never leaves this page.

On touch devices: left side of the arena is a virtual joystick, right side looks around (tap to fire), with dedicated fire / reload / pause buttons.
