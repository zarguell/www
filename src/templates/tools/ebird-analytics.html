eBird Field Analytics

Your eBird export as a fieldwork dashboard: migration waves, species phenology, effort, hotspots, and your life list. Parsing runs in your browser.

Load your data

STEP 1
  1. Open Download My Data on eBird and sign in.
  2. Click Request My Observations. eBird emails you a download link.
  3. Paste that link below. You do not need to open the zip.
Fetched through this site because eBird's storage sends no CORS headers, then unzipped locally. The link is a private credential and is never stored.

Working…

Nothing leaves your browser. A saved copy keeps your dashboard here so you do not need to request a new export from eBird; refresh it when you want this year's birds.

This is a shared list, so it carries each species and the month it was first seen. Checklists, effort, hotspots and monthly migration need a full export, so those views are not shown. Import your own data for the rest.
