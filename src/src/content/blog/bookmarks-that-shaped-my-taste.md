# Bookmarks That Shaped My Taste

I have a bookmark folder called "Taste Shapers." It contains links I've been returning to for years — sites that taught me something about design that I couldn't have learned any other way.

Here are some of them, and what they taught me.

## 1. Hacker News (The Minimalist Front Page)

**URL**: https://news.ycombinator.com

**What It Taught Me**: Information density doesn't have to be ugly. HN manages to pack an incredible amount of information onto a single page while remaining completely readable. The secret? Typographic hierarchy, generous whitespace between items, and ruthless consistency.

**Design Lesson**: You can have a lot of content without having a cluttered design. Space and structure matter more than minimalism.

## 2. GeoCities Archive (Random Pages)

**URL**: http://www.geocities.ws/

**What It Taught Me**: Personality beats polish. These old personal pages are technically rough, visually chaotic, and absolutely delightful. They have more character in one animated GIF than most modern sites have in their entire design system.

**Design Lesson**: Technical perfection is overrated. What matters is voice, perspective, and the courage to be weird.

## 3. A List Apart (Classic Articles)

**URL**: https://alistapart.com/article/oocss/

**What It Taught Me**: Design is about systems, not just pages. The classic ALA articles on object-oriented CSS changed how I think about design. A site isn't a collection of pages — it's a system of reusable components.

**Design Lesson**: Think in systems, not pages. Your design will be more consistent and more maintainable.

## 4. The Design of Everyday Things (Book Site)

**URL**: https://www.designofeverydaythings.com

**What It Taught Me**: Good design is invisible. Don Norman's work taught me that when design is working well, you don't notice it. You only notice design when it fails.

**Design Lesson**: Test your designs by observing when users *don't* notice the interface. That's success.

## 5. Brutal Websites (Archive)

**URL**: https://brutalwebsites.com (when it was active)

**What It Taught Me**: Web design doesn't have to follow app design conventions. We can be raw, unpolished, intentionally ugly. The web is its own medium with its own aesthetics.

**Design Lesson**: Don't default to "safe" design trends. The web rewards boldness and authenticity.

## 6. Solarized (Color Scheme)

**URL**: https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/

**What It Taught Me**: Restraint creates beauty. Ethan Schoonover created one of the most beloved color schemes by using precise color relationships and strict luminance ratios.

**Design Lesson**: Design systems before you design individual elements. The constraints you choose will shape the result more than your "creative decisions."

## 7. Personal Blogs of Great Designers

**URLs**: Too many to list

**What They Taught Me**: The best designers have strong personal voices. Their blogs aren't polished corporate presences — they're rough, opinionated, occasionally wrong, and always authentic.

**Design Lesson**: Your personal work should feel personal. Professional ≠ impersonal.

## The Common Thread

Looking at this list, I notice a pattern: The designs that shaped me aren't the slickest or most award-winning. They're the ones with **conviction**. They have a clear point of view and they don't try to be everything to everyone.

That's what I'm trying to do with this site: Make design decisions based on what I believe, not what's popular.
